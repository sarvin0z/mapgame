## Scope

Game UI of index.html: main menu, mission select and brief, in-game HUD, run result. Mode: operate (HUD, mission select), with the main menu's first viewport doing light persuade work.

## Audience and task

UEMR crew members choosing a call and driving it: they need the destination, its distance, the heading and their speed at a glance, and a run summary that teaches route knowledge.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Campus dispatch wayfinding. Every screen reads like the crew's dispatch board crossed with campus signage: a call is a card with a type, a location code, a distance and an ETA; in play, one band says where to go and how far.
WORLD: Dark warm glass panels (near-black, slightly warm, blurred) over the live 3D, UTD orange as the single action and highlight colour, UTD green for good/arrival, white humanist sans, tabular numerals. Pinned by the user's three reference boards.
FIRST VIEWPORT: The 3D campus behind; left a vertical nav column with the UTD EMS wordmark; centre a mission list of dispatch cards with photo thumbnails; right a brief panel with objectives and Start.
SIGNATURE: The followed destination stays highlighted from card to brief to HUD band to result, and the HUD distance counts down like a sign band.
RAISES: From airport wayfinding: one message per band, distances always shown, the followed destination is the only highlighted item. From split-flap boards: dispatch updates (next stop, events) change in place with a short flip, never pop.
RISK: Glass over a busy 3D scene can lose contrast; panels carry an opaque enough ground to keep 4.5:1 text contrast.
