    Controls

    The cart buttons beside the speedometer can be clicked too.

    On a touch screen, the stick at the bottom left drives: push up to accelerate, down to brake and then reverse, sideways to steer. The round buttons beside the map mute the sound and work the lights, siren, horn and headlights. Steering sensitivity is in Settings.

    W ↑Accelerate (brake when reversing)
    S ↓Brake, then reverse when stopped
    A DSteer
    SpacePark brake
    L H B KEmergency lights, siren, horn, headlights
    NMute or unmute all sound
    I CLocation card; camera (chase, driver, free)
    R ⌫Flip upright; return to start
    M P GMinimap, performance, graphics quality

    A run scores route efficiency (40%), pace (30%), staying on roads and walks (15%) and traffic (15%: slowing to 10 mph or less through stop signs, under 8 mph over speed bumps). Each pedestrian contact costs 50 points and each collision 100; the horn and siren clear people from your path. Arrive within 6 m of any wall of the building, or inside the lot. The best route and the destination beam cost 10% each.
